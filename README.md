# My Favorite Movies: The Lord of the Rings Trilogy

## About the Story
**The Lord of the Rings** is a fantasy movie trilogy directed by *Peter Jackson*. The story takes place in a magical world called Middle-earth. It follows a young hobbit named **Frodo Baggins**, who must go on a long and dangerous trip to destroy a powerful ring before the evil Dark Lord Sauron can get it back.

---

## Movies in the Trilogy
1. **The Fellowship of the Ring (2001)** – Frodo leaves his home and forms a team of friends to start the quest.
2. **The Two Towers (2002)** – The group splits up, and they face big battles while Frodo keeps walking toward Mount Doom.
3. **The Return of the King (2003)** – The final battle for Middle-earth happens, and Frodo finishes his mission.

## Why I Love These Movies
- **Great friendships:** Sam stays loyal to Frodo no matter how hard things get.
- **Amazing music and scenery:** The mountains, forests, and soundtrack make the world feel real.
- **Good vs. evil:** Even small and ordinary people can make a big difference.

## Favorite Quote
> "Even the smallest person can change the course of the future." — Galadriel
